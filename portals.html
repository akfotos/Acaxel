Portals

Four Portals. One Platform.

Every member of your school community has a dedicated, role-aware portal — with AI assistance, QR clock-in, real-time timetables, and a built-in support centre.

Parents

Parent Portal

Stay fully connected to your child's school life — fees, grades, assignments, and messages — in one place.

  • View and pay outstanding fees
  • Download invoices and receipts
  • Track attendance and grades
  • Monitor pending & overdue assignments
  • Message teachers directly
  • QR gate clock-in for school visits
  • AI Assistant for instant answers
  • Help & Support centre

Students

Student Portal

Access your full academic life — results, timetables, assignments, and daily attendance — all from one dashboard.

  • View grades and term results
  • Check daily & weekly timetable
  • See full attendance records

  • QR code check-in at school gate

  • AI Assistant for homework help
  • Help & Support centre

Every Portal Includes

Built-in for Everyone

Features every user gets, regardless of their role.

AI Assistant

Role-aware AI chat that answers questions, explains features, and guides navigation — no API key needed.

QR Clock-In

Scan the school gate QR code to mark arrival instantly. Manual code fallback included for every role.

Help & Support

Role-specific FAQ accordion, contact form, support ticket submission, and direct links to school contacts.

Dark Mode

Full light/dark theme support with automatic preference saving across all four portals.

How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

Getting started with Acaxel is simple. No technical setup required.

1. Create an Account

Sign up in under two minutes. Choose your role — parent, student, teacher, or admin.

2. Access Your Portal

Log in to your personalised dashboard. Your data, tabs, and tools are ready immediately.

3. Clock In at School

Scan the school gate QR code on arrival to mark your presence. On-time tracking is automatic.

4. Stay Connected

Manage fees, grades, timetables, messages, and more — ask the AI Assistant anytime you need help.
