Features

Everything Your School Needs

From fee management to grade tracking, Acaxel brings your entire school administration online.

Fee Management

Track tuition, levies, and outstanding balances. Generate invoices and send payment reminders automatically.

Grade Tracking

Administrators enter scores directly. Parents see real-time academic performance with subject breakdowns.

Attendance Records

Daily attendance marking with instant parent notifications for absences and late arrivals.

Direct Messaging

Secure in-app messaging between parents and administrators. No more missed notes or phone tag.

Billing & Invoices

Compose and send itemised bills to individual students or entire classes with one click.

Announcements

Broadcast school news, event reminders, and urgent notices to all parents and staff instantly.
