Core Features

Built for Every Part of School Life

From the gate to the classroom to the office — every workflow covered.

QR Code Clock-In

Teachers and students scan the school gate QR code to mark arrival instantly. Manual code fallback for when cameras aren't available. On-time vs. late status calculated automatically at 7:45 AM.

AI Assistant (Acaxel AI)

A role-aware AI chat widget on every portal. Answers questions about the dashboard, guides navigation, explains features, and provides instant support — available 24/7 without any API dependency.

Timetable Management

Admins build and publish class timetables. Teachers see their personal weekly schedule with a live "Up Next" banner. Automatic staff lesson reminders via in-app toast or browser notification.

Help & Support Centre

Every portal has a dedicated Support page with role-specific FAQ accordion, searchable help articles, a contact form for support tickets, and school contact details — all in one tab.

Fee Management

Track tuition, levies, and outstanding balances. Generate itemised invoices and send payment reminders. Parents can view and download receipts directly from their portal.

Grade Tracking

Teachers enter scores per subject. Parents and students see real-time academic performance with term breakdowns and grade trend charts updated live.

Attendance Records

Daily class attendance marking by teachers. Students track their own clock-in history via weekly presence grid. Full CSV export available for staff and student records.

Assignment Tracking

Parents monitor all their child's assignments filtered by status — Pending, Submitted, Overdue, or Graded. Students view full assignment details including due dates and subject.

Feeding & Canteen

Admins manage daily and weekly feeding menus, record per-student meal payments, and generate feeding collection reports — all within the dashboard.

Inventory Management

Track school stock — textbooks, stationery, equipment, and supplies. Add items, update quantities, monitor low-stock alerts, and export inventory reports as PDF.

Newsletters

Create richly formatted school newsletters with section editors. Save as drafts, export as PDF, mark as sent, and maintain a full publication archive — all from the admin dashboard.

Direct Messaging

Secure in-app conversations between parents, teachers, and administrators. No missed notes or phone tag — all communication logged in one place per user.

Billing & Invoices

Compose and send itemised bills to individual students or entire classes with one click. Parents download PDF invoices directly from their portal.

Announcements & Updates

Broadcast school news, event reminders, and urgent notices. Pin important posts, target specific audiences, and manage a full announcement archive.

Smart Notifications

In-app toasts and browser push notifications for lesson reminders, fee due dates, new grades, and school announcements — configurable per user and per role.

Dark Mode

Full light and dark theme support across all four portals. Preference saved automatically — no reset on reload.

PDF & CSV Exports

Export reports, invoices, newsletters, inventory lists, attendance logs, and timetables as formatted PDFs or CSV files — ready to print or share.

Fully Responsive

Works seamlessly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The sidebar collapses to a mobile menu, modals stack correctly, and the AI widget repositions automatically.
