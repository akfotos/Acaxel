Safeguarding Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Acaxel School is committed to providing a safe, secure, and supportive environment for all students, staff, parents, and visitors. This Safeguarding Policy outlines our approach to protecting children and vulnerable individuals from harm, abuse, and neglect.

1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

  • Protect children, young people, and vulnerable adults from harm.
  • Provide clear guidance to staff, parents, and students on safeguarding responsibilities.
  • Ensure prompt and appropriate action is taken when concerns arise.
  • Promote a culture of awareness, respect, and safety across the school community.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all members of the Acaxel School community, including:

  • Students and pupils
  • Teachers, administrators, and support staff
  • Parents, guardians, and visitors
  • Contractors, volunteers, and third-party service providers

3. Our Commitment

Acaxel School is committed to:

  • Treating every child with dignity and respect.
  • Creating an environment where children feel safe to speak up.
  • Recruiting staff responsibly, including background checks where required.
  • Responding quickly and fairly to any safeguarding concern.
  • Keeping accurate, confidential records of safeguarding incidents.
  • Regularly reviewing and updating this policy.

4. Types of Harm

Safeguarding covers all forms of harm and abuse, including but not limited to:

  • Physical abuse: hitting, shaking, or causing physical injury.
  • Emotional abuse: persistent criticism, humiliation, or threats.
  • Sexual abuse: inappropriate touching, exposure, or exploitation.
  • Neglect: failure to provide basic needs such as food, care, or supervision.
  • Bullying: repeated hurtful behaviour, including cyberbullying.
  • Online harm: grooming, exploitation, or exposure to harmful content.

5. Reporting Concerns

Anyone who has a safeguarding concern should report it immediately to a designated safeguarding lead or school administrator. Reports can be made:

  • In person at the school office
  • By phone at +233 55 6924 358
  • By email at safeguarding@acaxel.edu.gh
  • Through the school's official contact channels

All reports will be taken seriously, handled confidentially, and acted upon without delay.

6. Confidentiality and Record Keeping

Safeguarding information is sensitive and will be stored securely. Access is limited to authorised staff who need to know. Records are kept accurate, factual, and retained in line with legal and school requirements.

7. Online Safety

Acaxel promotes online safety through:

  • Supervised and age-appropriate use of digital platforms.
  • Clear rules on acceptable online behaviour.
  • Regular guidance for students, staff, and parents on safe internet use.
  • Prompt action against cyberbullying, inappropriate content, or online exploitation.

8. Training and Awareness

All staff receive safeguarding training as part of their onboarding and participate in regular refresher sessions. Students also receive age-appropriate lessons on personal safety, consent, and respectful behaviour.

9. Partnership with Parents

We believe safeguarding is a shared responsibility. Parents and guardians are encouraged to communicate openly with the school, attend safety workshops, and report any concerns promptly.

10. Review

This Safeguarding Policy is reviewed at least once every academic year or sooner if required by law, regulation, or a significant incident. Updates will be shared with staff, parents, and students.
