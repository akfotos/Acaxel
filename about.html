Core Features

Everything at a Glance

A powerful suite of tools built specifically for the realities of school management in Ghana and beyond.

QR Code Clock-In

Teachers and students scan the school gate QR code on arrival. On-time vs. late status is calculated automatically at 7:45 AM. Manual code fallback always available.

AI Assistant (Acaxel AI)

A floating role-aware AI chat on every portal. Answers questions, explains features, and guides users — available 24/7 with no external API dependency.

Timetable & Staff Alerts

Admins build class timetables that publish to teacher portals. Teachers get a live "Up Next" banner and automatic lesson reminders via browser or in-app notification.

Help & Support Centre

Every portal has a dedicated Support tab — role-specific FAQ accordion, searchable help articles, a contact form, and school contact details in one place.

Grades & Academic Tracking

Teachers enter scores per subject. Students and parents see live grade trend charts, term breakdowns, and subject performance at a glance.

Fee & Financial Management

Manage tuition, levies, and balances. Generate itemised invoices, track payments, and let parents download PDF receipts directly from their portal.

Assignment Tracking

Parents and students monitor all assignments filtered by status — Pending, Submitted, Overdue, or Graded — with due dates and subject details.

Feeding & Inventory

Admins manage weekly feeding menus, record meal payments, track school stock levels, and export feeding and inventory reports as PDF.

Newsletters & Announcements

Create richly formatted newsletters, broadcast school-wide announcements, pin important notices, and maintain a full archive — all from the admin dashboard.

Who We Serve

Built for Everyone in Your School

Whether you sit in the principal's office or check your child's grades from your phone, Acaxel was designed with you in mind.

For Administrators

Manage students, staff, fees, feeding, inventory, timetables, newsletters, and announcements from one powerful dashboard. Built-in AI assistant and full PDF/CSV export on every report.

For Teachers

Clock in with a QR scan, view your weekly timetable with a live "Up Next" banner, take class attendance, enter grades, message parents, and export your attendance log as CSV.

For Students

Check in at the school gate by QR scan, view grades and timetables, track assignments by status, message teachers, and check fee balances — all from one mobile-friendly portal.

For Parents

Stay on top of fees, invoices, grades, attendance, and assignments. Message teachers directly, receive school announcements, and use the QR clock-in for school visits.

AI Assistant — Every Role

Acaxel AI is built into every portal. It detects your role and tailors its answers — guiding admins through settings, helping teachers with clock-in, and supporting parents with fee queries.

Help & Support — Everyone

Every user gets a full Support tab with role-specific FAQs, a contact form, support ticket submission, and direct school contact details — no hunting for help.
