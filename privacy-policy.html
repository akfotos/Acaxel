Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Acaxel School ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our platform — parents, students, teachers, administrators, and visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us provide and improve the Acaxel platform:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, role (parent, student, teacher, admin), and password.
  • School information: school name, school type, school code, and staff position.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, device type, browser, and IP address.
  • School records: attendance, grades, fee payments, invoices, messages, and other data entered by authorised users.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage user accounts and school profiles.
  • Provide features such as fee tracking, grade management, attendance, and messaging.
  • Communicate important school updates, announcements, and support responses.
  • Improve platform performance, security, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share data:

  • Within your school: teachers, administrators, and parents may see information relevant to their role.
  • With service providers: trusted partners who help us host, secure, and maintain the platform.
  • For legal reasons: when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, and regular monitoring to protect your data. However, no online service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Choices

You can update your account information at any time through your profile settings. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, subject to school and legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Acaxel uses browser cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and improve performance. You can manage or clear these through your browser settings at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Student accounts are created and managed by the school or parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@acaxel.edu.gh or through our Contact page.
